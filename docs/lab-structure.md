# Lab guide structure

Each lab uses the following sections. Shared installation steps belong in [common setup](00-common-setup.md).

1. **Overview:** target, scenario, learning objective, completion criteria and verification status.
2. **Before you begin:** lab-specific accounts, tools, starting state and safety limits.
3. **Understand the scenario:** the relevant feature and security boundary.
4. **Guided walkthrough:** prepare, capture a normal baseline, investigate, perform a controlled test and verify the outcome. Each step says what to do and what to look for.
5. **Hints and troubleshooting:** optional guidance and known obstacles.
6. **Evidence:** sanitized material and findings to submit.
7. **Cleanup and review:** restore changed state and answer questions about impact and remediation.

Screenshots, durations and observed results require a pilot. Label source-derived expectations as unverified until the exercise has been run.
